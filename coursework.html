About Uganda Martyrs University

Uganda Martyrs University is a faith-based private universityfounded on the principles of Virtue and Hard work.Located atNkozi,we are committed to academic excellence,ethicalvalues and service to society through teaching,research and community engagement.

Steps for Student Registration

  1. Receive admission letter from Uganda Martyrs
  2. Complete the Student RegistrationForm provided below
  3. Submit academic documents and national ID to the Academic Registrar
  4. Pay tuition fees and obtain your University Student ID Card
